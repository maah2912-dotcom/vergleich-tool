## What Apple's 0/10 Repairability Score Really Means for You

When iFixit tore down the AirPods Pro 3, they didn't mince words: **0 out of 10 for repairability**. Not 3. Not 2. Zero — the lowest possible score, reserved for devices that are essentially impossible to fix without destroying them in the process.

But does that mean you shouldn't buy them? The answer depends entirely on who you are.

## What "0/10" Actually Means

iFixit had to essentially destroy the AirPods Pro 3 just to look inside them. Here's what they found:

- **Battery sealed with glue** — the same design flaw present since the very first AirPods in 2016. Nothing has changed.
- **No way to open the case without damage** — heat is required to melt the adhesive, and the flex cable inside each earbud makes reassembly nearly impossible.
- **Charging case equally unrepairable** — the 1.334 Wh battery inside the case is also glued in.
- **Most repair shops refuse to touch them** — battery replacements are so complex that many professional technicians won't even attempt the job.

The moment the battery wears out, the AirPods Pro 3 become e-waste.

## The Battery Reality Check

Lithium-ion batteries degrade over time. After 2-3 years of daily use, most earbud batteries hold 70-80% of their original capacity. For AirPods Pro 3, that means:

- **Year 1-2:** Full performance, great battery life
- **Year 2-3:** Noticeable degradation, shorter listening sessions
- **Year 3+:** Your €209 earbuds are effectively disposable

Apple does offer a battery service — but at €89 per earbud, replacing both earbuds and the case can cost more than buying a new pair.

## The EU Right-to-Repair Context

The EU's Right to Repair Directive pushes manufacturers toward longevity and repairability. Apple's AirPods design philosophy moves in the exact opposite direction. While earbuds aren't yet covered explicitly, the regulatory pressure is mounting — and this debate will only get louder.

## What the Alternatives Look Like

Not all premium earbuds are built this way:

**Sony WF-1000XM6 (~€259)** — Class-leading audio and ANC, no ecosystem lock-in. Better long-term value for non-Apple users.

**Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro (~€179)** — Anker's new flagship with comparable ANC at €30 less. More affordable to replace if needed.

**EarFun Air Pro 4 (~€80)** — If you accept earbuds as consumables, €80 every 3-4 years is smarter economics than €209 for an unrepairable product.

## Should You Buy the AirPods Pro 3?

**Yes, if:**
- You're deep in the Apple ecosystem (iPhone, Mac, Apple Watch)
- You treat earbuds as a 2-3 year product and budget accordingly
- You want best-in-class ANC and seamless device switching

**No, if:**
- You own Android or a mixed-device setup
- Sustainability matters to you
- You want 4-5 years from a premium purchase

## The Bottom Line

The AirPods Pro 3 are exceptional earbuds with a deliberate design limitation. The 0/10 repairability score reflects Apple's choice to prioritize thinness over longevity. Go in with clear eyes: you're paying for a 2-3 year premium experience, not a long-term investment.
